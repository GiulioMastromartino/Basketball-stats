HoopsLab — Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 September 2026 · App: HoopsLab (basketball statistics and training management)

1. What this app does

HoopsLab lets coaches manage teams, training sessions, games, and play diagrams, and optionally copy exported PDFs into the coach's own Google Drive.

2. Google data we access, and why

Only if you click “Connect Google Drive”, we request a single OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — create and manage only the files HoopsLab itself creates in your Drive. We cannot see, open, or modify any other file in your Drive.

We use it solely to create a HoopsLab/<Team>/<type>/ folder in your Drive and upload PDF exports you request (training session PDFs today; other export types only after you enable them).

We do not read your email, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.

3. What we store

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, encrypted at rest (Fernet) — never in plaintext, never in logs.
  • Your Google account email (to show “Connected as …”), sync preferences, and the Drive file IDs of your own uploaded copies (to update instead of duplicating them).

Exported PDF bytes are passed through memory at upload time and are never persisted on our servers.

4. What we never do

  • No sale, rental, or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No advertising use of your data.
  • No access to files in your Drive that HoopsLab did not create.
  • No background scanning of your Drive.

5. Deletion and disconnect

You can disconnect at any time via HoopsLab → Google Drive → Disconnect. This immediately deletes our copy of your tokens, preferences, and sync state. Files already uploaded remain in your Drive under your control — delete them there if you wish. Deleting your HoopsLab account deletes the same records.

6. Data retention

Tokens and sync state are kept only while your Drive stays connected, to perform the uploads you asked for. On disconnect or account deletion they are removed as described above.

7. Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest, transported over HTTPS, and refreshed server-side. Drive failures never expose token material to other users.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the HoopsLab administrator at the support address published on the Google OAuth consent screen for this app.
