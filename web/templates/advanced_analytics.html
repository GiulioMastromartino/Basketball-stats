Four Factors Explanation
eFG% (Effective FG%)

Measures shooting efficiency, accounting for 3-pointers being worth more.

eFG% = (FGM + 0.5*3PM) / FGA
TOV% (Turnover %)

Measures how often possessions end in turnovers.

TOV% = TOV / Possessions
ORB% (Offensive Rebound %)

Measures offensive rebounding effectiveness.

ORB% = OREB / Total Rebounds
FT Rate (Free Throw Rate)

Measures ability to get to the free throw line.

FT Rate = FTM / FGA
