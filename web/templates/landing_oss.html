Three pillars for game day.

HOOPSLAB replaces the messy stack of tools with one narrative: track the game live, understand what's happening, then print and share the story instantly.

Live Command Center

Replace pen-and-paper tracking. Log events fast, tag plays in the moment, and keep score context accurate from tip-off to the final possession.

  • Event logging + possession context
  • Play tagging (selectors, filters)
  • Halftime-ready summaries

Automatic Intelligence

Real-time TS%, eFG%, Net Rating and lineup impact — computed continuously so decisions happen during the game, not on Monday in a spreadsheet.

  • Advanced box score metrics
  • Lineup + duo/trio combinations
  • Player trends + scouting outputs

Print & Share

Server-side PDFs that look like coaching documents — not browser printouts. Export halftime, game, player, lineup, season and clutch reports in one click.

Then deliver them automatically: players and staff get the report on WhatsApp or by email the moment the game ends — no copying, no forwarding.

  • Halftime + game summaries
  • Player reports + scouting cards
  • Team bundles + ZIP exports
  • Auto-share via WhatsApp or email
